    Every road that crosses a stream squeezes it through a pipe.

    Hurricane Helene damaged nearly 9,500 sites on western North Carolina's state roads, 867 of them culverts by NCDOT's early count. NCDOT now puts transportation recovery near $5.8 billion.

    Undersized culverts fail twice. In floods they plug and wash the road out. The rest of the time they wall rivers off from the fish downstream. Culverts built to stream width fix both, and they came through Tropical Storm Irene intact where undersized pipes failed.

    Money to replace them is limited. Pinchpoint chooses which ones to replace, treating the river as a network instead of ranking pipes one at a time.
